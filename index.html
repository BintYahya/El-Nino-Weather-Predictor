EL NINO AND LA NINA


According to the World Meteorological Organization (WMO), El Niño and La Niña are two opposite phases of a naturally occuring climate pattern known as the El Niño Climate Oscillation (ENSO), involving fluctuating ocean temperatures in the central and equitorial Pacific, coupled with changes in the overlying atmosphere.
